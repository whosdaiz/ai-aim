MAGNET

Aim director

Mode Pulls whenever a picked bone is inside FOV.
Key Press to turn the pull on or off.
Bones
Pull toward —
Curve How pull holds from the FOV edge onto the bone. Ease out / expo keep more of it close-in.

BEHAVIOR

How it works

Activates

When one of the selected bones on a freshly detected player enters its FOV, and the Mode allows it: always, after pressing the key (Toggle), or while the key is held (Hold).

Changes

Pulls toward the closest selected bone. Move toward a different selected bone and the pull switches to it, then stays there when you stop. Force is the closing rate (100% is the previous 50%), Curve holds more of that pull as you get closer, and Max force caps each update.

Helps

Guides you onto the bone. Ease out / Ease out expo stay stronger up close; Ease in waits until you are near the bone. Deadzone leaves the last few pixels alone.

Releases

Stops inside the deadzone, the instant you move away, at the FOV edge, and never steps through the aim point.

LEADING

Target prediction

Prediction moves the aim point for every module that aims: director, corrector, tracking, spray, tap, one tap, trigger and magnet all follow it. Turn on Prediction guide in the Visual tab to see the leaded box on screen.

BEHAVIOR

How it works

The problem

A player's position on screen moves both when they move and when you turn your view. Leading on the raw screen motion would aim into empty space every time you flick.

Your view

Mouse counts are read through Raw Input and converted to pixels, which is how far a standing still player appears to slide. Subtracting that leaves only the player's own motion.

The check

With three or more players on screen the view turn is measured from how they shift together, needing no calibration, and that corrects the counts-to-pixels number for scoping or a wrong DPI.

Backs off

Speed comes from a fit over several frames, not two, so box jitter does not become false motion. Confidence falls on flicks, target switches and still players, and the lead shrinks with it.

PATH

Aim corrector

Bones
Correct around —

BEHAVIOR

How it works

Activates

When the selected bone is in FOV, ahead of your current mouse path, and close enough to be a plausible correction rather than a new target.

Changes

Bends the path toward the selected bone's height as soon as it is inside FOV, up to Max bend. Correction is how much of that bend is used. It adds no forward acceleration, and the bend eases off as the heights meet.

Helps

Does most of the height correction early, then leaves the rest of the flick straight once you are at the bone's height.

Releases

When stationary, moving past or behind the target, outside FOV, on a wide turn, or when detection becomes stale.

RETENTION

Tracking assist

BEHAVIOR

How it works

Activates

Only after the center of the same player box stays inside Hold radius for the full Settle time and your mouse begins drifting outward slowly.

Changes

Cancels only the configured fraction of outward movement. It never reverses that movement and never generates movement at rest.

Helps

Softens tiny tracking slips after you have already acquired the player; it does not find or follow targets for you.

Releases

Immediately at Breakaway speed, outside Hold radius, after a target switch, or when detection becomes stale.

SPRAY

Spray assist

Switch delay After the spray moves to a different player, wait a random time in this range before sliding onto them. Both ends at 0 switches straight away.
0 ms to 0 ms

BEHAVIOR

How it works

Activates

Only while left mouse is physically held, Spray assist is enabled, and the closest player is inside FOV.

Changes

Applies a light, frame-rate-independent slide on X only toward that player's center. Recoil and vertical aim stay entirely yours. Force sets the closing rate and Max force caps each update.

Helps

Walks the crosshair onto the closest player during a spray, then sits still so your own tracking and spray control take over.

Switching

The first player of a spray is taken straight away. If the spray then moves to a different player, Switch delay holds it back for a random time from that range before it slides onto the new one. Releasing left click clears the wait, so the next spray starts fresh.

Releases

Stops as soon as you are horizontally on the player, eases in after mouse-down, yields if you aim away, and never steps through the aim point.

RP2040

One tap assist

On an armed left-down the Feather withholds the click from the game and Argus starts easing onto the head. A tap (release inside Hold window) then clicks; a hold is released as a spray.

BEHAVIOR

How it works

Activates

Only while the head is inside FOV, cooldown is clear, capture is running, and the RP2040 is connected. Unarmed clicks are never delayed.

Changes

Withholds an armed left-down on the Feather. The crosshair starts easing onto the head on press. A tap (release inside Hold window) is consumed and a click is injected after the snap. A hold past the window is forwarded as a spray with no injected click.

Helps

Cleans a first tap onto the head without eating sprays or rapid follow-up shots on the same player.

Releases

Does not intercept again until Cooldown expires or the head leaves Reset FOV. Firmware auto-disarms within 300ms if the app or link dies, so clicks cannot stay swallowed.

CLICK

Tap assist

Bones
Nudge toward —

BEHAVIOR

How it works

Activates

Once on the rising edge of a physical left-click when the selected bone is inside FOV. Holding the button does not repeat it.

Changes

Adds one movement toward the selected bone, capped by Max nudge and never allowed to step past the current aim point.

Helps

Cleans up a small final error at shot time without continuously steering your normal mouse movement.

Optional

Nudge trigger clicks applies the same one-shot correction to clicks sent by Trigger or Magnet trigger.

FIRE

Trigger

Bones
Fire on —
Interval Waits a random time in this range between hold-fire clicks. Set both ends the same for a fixed wait.
295 ms to 360 ms

BEHAVIOR

How it works

Activates

Only while the Hold key is physically held and the crosshair overlaps a selected Fire on bone plus its FOV allowance.

Changes

Waits out the Initial delay after landing on a bone, then sends left-clicks no faster than a random wait picked from the Interval range each time, so the pace does not lock into a fixed rhythm. Trigger itself never moves or pulls the cursor.

Helps

Handles shot timing after you place the crosshair, while leaving acquisition and tracking completely manual.

Combines

Can run beside Magnet trigger independently. Tap's Nudge trigger clicks option may add a shot-time nudge if enabled.

PULL

Magnet trigger

Bones
Fire on —
Pull toward —
Interval Waits a random time in this range between magnet hold-fire clicks. Set both ends the same for a fixed wait.
295 ms to 360 ms

BEHAVIOR

How it works

Activates

Only while its Hold key is physically held. Pull and firing use their own independently selected bones and FOV values.

Changes

Applies a smoothed pull toward the nearest eligible Pull toward bone, then clicks when a Fire on bone enters the fire zone.

Helps

Combines short-range acquisition and shot timing in one held action. Speed is the close rate in pixels, Force is how much of it is used, and Smoothness softens the pull.

Releases

Immediately when the key is released or no pull bone is in FOV. Trigger can remain enabled separately with its own bind and values.

RP2040

Aim assist

Hardware slowdown: while the crosshair is over a selected bone, the Feather scales your mouse's own movement down before Windows ever sees it.

Bones
Slow on —

BEHAVIOR

How it works

Activates

While the crosshair is on a selected Slow on bone (plus Bone margin) and detection is fresh. Needs capture running and the RP2040 connected.

Changes

Multiplies the physical mouse's X/Y deltas inside the firmware. Ramp in eases that factor down; Ramp out eases it back when you leave a bone. Sub-count remainders are carried, so slow movement stays smooth instead of stuttering. Nothing is ever added.

Helps

Classic aim-slowdown feel, limited to the bones you pick: crossing a head or chest becomes sticky because your own hand movement is geared down over them.

Releases

Eases back over Ramp out when the crosshair leaves every selected bone. Instantly if Release on shot is on and left mouse is held. The firmware also auto-restores full speed within 300ms if the app or link ever dies mid-slowdown.

Using Windows API

Windows API uses SendInput. RP2040 sends everything to the Feather over a USB HID command channel instead (one USB cable, no serial adapter), so movement and clicks come from a real HID mouse — and it unlocks Aim assist and One tap assist, which rewrite the physical mouse's packets in hardware. All other modules work the same on both. Applied on capture start.

Show prices?

Argus can estimate what each item and your whole inventory are worth, using the lowest CSFloat buy-now listing for that item and wear. This needs a free CSFloat API key.

CS2 stats come from Leetify. They work without a key, but Leetify applies stricter rate limits. You can create a free key on the Leetify developer page and add it now or later in Settings.
